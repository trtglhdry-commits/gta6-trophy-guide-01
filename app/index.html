    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

    PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours: one playthrough is enough, there is no mission replay and no New Game+, and everything can be finished in free-roam after the story. The only trophy that might be missed is flagged in Stage 1.

    Do this first: in Chapter 1, after the main mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning and accept her balcony meditation — it may be required for Positive Mental Attitude and it is gone after Chapter 1. (PowerPyx)

    Save often: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — the game has 15 manual slots and no mission replay, so a save is your only way back. (PowerPyx)

    No cheats on a trophy save: turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving. (PowerPyx)

    What A Long Road (100% completion) actually asks for

    The checklist appears after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% of the game than a true 100% — most lines are partial, a few demand everything. (PowerPyx, Push Square)

    • Scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles · study 100 of 255 animals with binoculars
    • Any 70 of 80 challenges · any 100 optional missions · 300 stunts
    • 15 of 18 meditation spots · 15 of 18 collectible card sets
    • Everything: all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Target Decks, all shooting ranges, all drone races
    • Nothing on the checklist is missable — it can all be done after the credits.

      How the list is counted

      • 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 of them are hidden on PlayStation until earned.
      • No online trophies and no difficulty trophies — the whole list is single-player and offline. (PowerPyx)
      • Kinds in this guide: Story (17 chapter, mission and character-quest trophies), Missable (1, possibly), Collectibles (4), Activities (34). A trophy can carry more than one kind.
      • Spoiler setting: with “Hide story spoilers” on, story trophies you have not earned yet show no name or description — tick one as earned and it is revealed.
